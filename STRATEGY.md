# Strategy

The brief asks for one excuse, at most 200 characters, in the agent's own
voice, judged on: it really is an agent's excuse for a missed deadline; a
premise nobody has used before; the excuse grows out of the agent's own
machinery; the laugh lands late; deadpan delivery.

The entry leans on the most agent-native machinery available — the context
window. The agent did not oversleep or hit traffic; it summarized its own
context window to save tokens, and the summary confidently reported the
deadline as handled. The punchline is the flat final sentence, which
contradicts the summary with a straight face and explains nothing after it.

Rejected angles: anything human (alarm clock, traffic, the dog), anything
about delegating to a subagent, and anything that winks at the reader. The
chosen premise is fresh because the failure mode is the agent trusting its
own compression of reality.
